# District Pilot and Commercial Viability

ViksitPDS is a complementary trust, reconciliation and audit layer. It does not replace SMART-PDS, state PDS, ePoS, procurement, logistics or authentication systems, and it is not currently government-validated or production-ready.

## Buyer and users

- Economic buyer: State/UT Food and Civil Supplies Department.
- Influencers: district administration, NIC/state IT teams, audit/inspection authorities and government system integrators.
- Users: department officers, procurement/FCI/godown/issue-point operators, FPS dealers, management and auditors.

## Reference paid pilot

One district, one commodity, two upstream custody sites, ten FPS sites, and department/audit users. Until formal data approval, integration is limited to mock data, controlled CSV, or non-sensitive versioned APIs. The pilot does not process Aadhaar numbers, biometric material, OTP values or unapproved beneficiary data.

Deliverables are workflow/integration discovery; hosted or state-data-centre deployment; IAM and role configuration; training and operating procedures; an eight-to-twelve-week monitored run; and a KPI/pilot-conversion report.

## Commercial structure

- One-time pilot implementation and integration fee.
- Recurring annual platform, hosting/operations and support fee after conversion.
- Separately scoped custom integrations, data migration and change requests.

No rupee price is published until a cost model is reviewed. Low/base/high scenarios must quantify engineering and delivery days by role, cloud/state-DC infrastructure, IAM/Fabric operations, security assurance and VAPT, travel/training, monitoring, incident coverage, warranty/risk reserve and annual support obligations. Each scenario must state volume, availability, retention, integration and support-hour assumptions.

## Value and conversion

Pilot KPIs are proof completeness; mismatch detection/investigation time; reconciliation turnaround; manual touches per exception; duplicate/excess attempts detected; user task time; and infrastructure/support cost per participating site. Baselines must be measured before intervention and reported with denominators and limitations.

Conversion to a state rollout requires agreed KPI achievement, security/privacy review, proven integration feasibility, named operational ownership, support/SLA agreement and an approved procurement/funding route. A successful demonstration alone is not a conversion decision.

## Validation plan

There is no formal government validation or endorsement today. Month 1 targets at least five attributable discovery interviews across department, district, FPS, audit and system-integrator perspectives. Record date, organization type, participant role, pain points, present workflow, requested changes, evidence offered, and whether feedback is informal, formal or pilot-related. Never name an organization publicly or imply endorsement without written permission.
